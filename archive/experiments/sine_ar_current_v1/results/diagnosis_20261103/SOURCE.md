# Provenance and scope

This is the threshold/temperature diagnosis requested after publication
of commit `c010f9e2de0ef4ada8c3df1767f85e38c9b0ed32`.
The original run at `../run_20261103/` remains unchanged.

`protocol.json` was saved before training or calibration. `diagnose_source.py`
is the executed diagnostic driver snapshot. `bank_audit.json` records the
SHA-256 hashes of every original source-attempt file. All thresholds reuse
those masks, empirical query errors, and original heldout-card assignments.
The strict-bank checkpoints and training histories are copied verbatim.

Other checkpoints use frequency-weighted likelihood on unique masks,
equivalent to full-batch training on all accepted cards. The MADE model
and the numerical solver are the original implementations. The maintained
solver only changes the artifact filename construction in `save_fit`.

`distribution_*.npz` arrays enumerate binary masks in integer order, with
position zero as the least significant bit. Rows follow the temperature
order in `protocol.json`, with raw AR followed by AR-budget2 for each
temperature. Corresponding JSON files provide probability summaries.

Calibration stores final candidate masks, weights, empirical query losses,
and population diagnostics. Hyperparameter selection uses query only.
Four strict-bank temperature scores are reused from the original JSON
selection record; their old NPZ files suffered the documented name collision.
All new calibration NPZ filenames preserve the entire decimal temperature
and seed. No missing old calibration grids were silently reconstructed.

The fresh-function stage begins after `selection_frozen.json` is written.
It preserves final candidates and query-selected solutions for all seeds.
Intermediate inner-optimizer histories are not exported in this diagnostic
run; the original experiment retains its histories. `comparison.csv` and
`summary.json` are generated by the report script from these final records.

`source_task00_per_output.npz` repeats one original source task to recover
per-output risks. Its 16384 fits are diagnostic only and never enter the
training banks. `partial_solution_audit.json` records the exact agreement
with the original query errors and the additional computation.

This is development evidence on a previously explored structural split.
New numerical functions do not make it a confirmatory test on unseen
structural families. The relaxed bank already contains some or all of the
previously withheld complete masks. Final success remains MSE below 0.1.
