Inductive biases shape how neural networks generalize from limited data, yet encoding them in network connectivity often relies on domain expertise.
This paper presents a generative approach that draws on related tasks to discover shared structural constraints.
For each task, a generative model approximates a family of hypothesis spaces that admit near-optimal solutions.
Intersecting these task-specific families narrows the search to structures compatible with all tasks, automating the discovery of a shared inductive bias (want lossless).
