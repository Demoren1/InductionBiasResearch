Inductive biases play a central role in the generalization and data efficiency of neural networks.
Network connectivity provides one way to encode such biases, but is often specified manually.
We propose a generative approach to discovering inductive biases shared across related tasks.
For each task, we learn a generative model of network connectivity structures from trained networks.
We formulate shared structure discovery as a search for a common connectivity pattern within the families represented by these task-specific models.
