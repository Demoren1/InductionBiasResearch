Inductive biases play a central role in the generalization and data efficiency of neural networks.
Encoding these assumptions through network connectivity often relies on domain expertise.
This paper presents a generative approach to discovering connectivity patterns shared across related tasks.
For each task, a separate generative model captures structural patterns extracted from trained networks.
These models are used to refine the constraints shared across tasks.
The central hypothesis is that agreement between them reveals structural inductive biases that generalize to previously unseen tasks.
