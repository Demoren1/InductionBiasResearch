Inductive biases shape how neural networks generalize from limited data, yet encoding them in network connectivity often relies on domain expertise.
We propose a generative approach that represents solutions to related tasks in a shared latent space.
Our central hypothesis is that this shared representation encourages overlap between regions corresponding to structures useful for different tasks, implicitly revealing their common structural constraints.
We optimize the latent code to identify a shared connectivity mask that can serve as a transferable inductive bias for learning new tasks.
