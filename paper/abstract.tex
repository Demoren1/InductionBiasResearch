The connectivity structure of a neural network influences how well it generalizes from limited data, yet designing this structure often requires domain expertise.
We propose a generative multi-task learning framework that learns a distribution over network structures from solutions to related tasks.
Our central hypothesis is that structural regularities shared across these solutions provide an inductive bias that transfers to new tasks.
We generate structures autoregressively, specifying both connectivity and weight sharing, and train their numerical parameters from scratch on each new task.
Experiments on a synthetic signal-detection benchmark show that transferring these structures improves generalization over tuned fully connected baselines.
