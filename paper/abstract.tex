Inductive biases play a central role in the generalization and data efficiency of neural networks.
Network connectivity encodes such biases, yet its design often relies on domain expertise.
This paper presents a generative approach to discovering connectivity patterns shared across related tasks.
For each task, a separate generative model captures a family of connectivity patterns extracted from trained networks.
These generative models are used to refine the inductive bias shared across tasks.
The central hypothesis is that agreement across task-specific generative models reveals structural inductive biases that generalize to previously unseen tasks.
