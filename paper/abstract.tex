We study how fitted solutions to related tasks can reveal weight-sharing
groups for learning new tasks from limited data. We characterize when
aligned ReLU weights identify these groups and how partial information
from several tasks can resolve individual ambiguities. We propose
Approximate Sign--Ratio Recovery (aSRR), which clusters profiles of
normalized source weights. We reuse the assignments and learn fresh
code values on each new task. In the synthetic experiment, full-support
assignments miss the sparse teacher's indexator. On UCI HAR and
Omniglot, aSRR improves over Dense by 5.07 and 2.11--2.18
percentage points, respectively. On Omniglot, aSRR constructs groups
faster than WSR from saved Dense fits at both tested widths. Their
accuracies are similar at 1024 codes, and WSR leads at 4096.
